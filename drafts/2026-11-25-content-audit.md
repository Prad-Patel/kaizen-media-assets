## What is working

1. **Showing a real thing we built beats explaining a tool.** Every top post on LinkedIn and Instagram is a real build on screen: Hampden Road sales site (LinkedIn 13.6k), CV website (LinkedIn 3.4k), quote calculator (LinkedIn 1,027), extension planner (IG 143, LinkedIn 522), Worktop Compare £7m (IG 139), £20k platform (TikTok 747, LinkedIn 263).
2. **Native video on Prad's LinkedIn has broken out.** The two video posts (18 and 21 Nov) did 3,398 and 13,632 impressions. The last nine carousels did 52 to 315 and are trending down (Gemini Black Friday carousel, 23 Nov: 52). The runbook's "video is not buying reach on LinkedIn" was measured on the company page, not the personal profile, and no longer holds.
3. **Real screen footage holds attention on Instagram.** Hampden Road: 51% skip and 20.7s average watch, against 80 to 95% skip and 2 to 5s for illustration-and-type videos. Gmail connectors walkthrough: 55% skip, 12.6s watch.
4. **TikTok photo posts.** The one photo post (Claude models infographic) did 3x the TikTok floor and the most likes of the period. Matches the July test (photo carousels 718 to 753 views).
5. **Prad's personal LinkedIn grows every week** (+73 in two months). It is the only account where posts reach new people.

## What is not working

1. **Statistic and fear hooks.** Lowest Instagram reels: AI bill shock (22), sensitive data (18), late payments £26bn (3), scam 395% (3), cyber incidents (10). They are now banned by the READ FIRST rule, and the data backs it.
2. **Kaizen page reshares.** 0 to 8 impressions each. Not worth the step.
3. **Comments.** Almost none anywhere despite every caption ending on a question. Questions alone are not producing conversation.
4. **TikTok converts nobody.** Around 650 views a post with zero follower growth in two months. The views look like TikTok's baseline test audience, not people choosing us.
5. **Generic how-to carousels with stock illustrations** are flat on every channel lately (Gemini posts IG 29 / LinkedIn 52; Grok IG 26; review replies IG 12 / LinkedIn 106).

## Recommendations

1. Lead each week with at least one **real build shown on screen** (a screen recording of the actual tool, a voiceover, a CTA end card), posted as native video on Prad's LinkedIn. Use builds we have (artifacts, client tools, our own pipeline), or build a small one live for the how-to.
2. **On how-to days, record the tool being used** (a screen capture of the prompt going in and the result coming out) instead of a stills carousel. Keep the PDF carousel only as an optional extra.
3. **Test TikTok photo carousels** for two weeks on how-to days (slides as a photo post rather than a carousel video) and compare with the 650 floor.
4. **Drop the Kaizen page reshare** and treat the company page and X as low-effort mirrors.
5. **Swap the closing question for a comment prompt with a reward**, for example "Comment ROTA and I'll send you the detailed prompt", and measure comments for two weeks.
6. Re-pull these numbers on about 9 December and compare.
