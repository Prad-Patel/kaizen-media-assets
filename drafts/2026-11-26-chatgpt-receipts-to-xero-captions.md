ChatGPT now does your bookkeeping. Xero's connection is US-only for now, so here's the UK version 👇

A month of receipts into Xero in about 20 minutes:
1. Photograph your receipts (or save the PDFs)
2. Download Xero's bill import template and your chart of accounts
3. Upload it all to ChatGPT with this prompt:

"Read each receipt I have uploaded and fill one row per receipt in the Xero bill import template, using only the account codes and tax types in my chart of accounts. Mark any row you are not sure of with CHECK and a short reason. Give me the finished file as a CSV to download."

4. Check the rows marked CHECK, then import

60 receipts used to be 3 hours of typing. That's about 32 hours a year back (our example). The detailed prompt is on our blog, link in bio.
