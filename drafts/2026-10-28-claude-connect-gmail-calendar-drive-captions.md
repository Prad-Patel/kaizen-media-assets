## LinkedIn (personal profile, carousel PDF; Kaizen page reshares)

Claude can sort your inbox, find a free slot in your diary and draft a quote from your own price list, once you connect it to Gmail, Calendar and Drive.

Setup takes about ten minutes:

1. In Claude settings, open Connectors and click Connect next to Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive.
2. Sign in to Google and approve.
3. Try one job per connector. The prompts are on slides 3 to 5.

The Gmail one: "List emails from the last two days that need a reply from me, most urgent first. Draft replies to the top three and save them as drafts. Do not send."

You read the drafts and press send yourself. Per Anthropic's help pages, Claude asks for your approval before sending email or sharing files by default.

As an illustration, ten minutes a day hunting for emails, slots and files is about 40 hours over 48 weeks. Time your own week to check.

Which takes most of your week: the inbox, the diary or the paperwork?
