## LinkedIn (personal profile, carousel PDF)

76% of UK small business owners now make or edit social posts every week (Markel Direct, 501 owners). Black Friday week is when we'd tell any client to stop doing it in scraps.

Do it in one sitting instead. Gemini makes that easier now: Google has added a business page to the Gemini app where you save your brand style once, then every image it makes after that uses your logo, colours and fonts.

How we'd run it this morning:

1. Save your brand style: logo, colours, fonts, or a clean brand sheet.
2. Paste one prompt that plans five posts for Monday to Friday around your real offer.
3. Edit the plan, then make each image in your saved style.
4. Add prices and dates as real text, not in the picture.
5. Schedule all five in one go.

The full prompt is on slide 4. One rule: check every price against your till before it goes out.
