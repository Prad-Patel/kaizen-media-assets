## LinkedIn (personal profile, carousel PDF; reach test 3: no firstComment at publish)

We'd tell any client with a Google Business Profile the same thing: answer every review, and let ChatGPT do the first draft.

85% of UK shoppers check at least one review site before they buy (Feefo and Censuswide, 3,000 UK consumers). They read the stars, then they read how you replied. Most owners know this and still leave reviews hanging, because every reply feels like a fresh bit of writing.

The fix takes about 10 minutes a week:

1. Write a house style once: your tone, under 60 words, thank them by first name, mention one thing they said.
2. Copy the week's new reviews from Business Profile.
3. Paste them into ChatGPT with one batch prompt and get every draft at once.
4. Use a separate prompt for the one-star review: own it, no arguing, take it offline.
5. Edit, then post.

The prompts are on the slides. Works on the free plan, because you only paste text. Read every reply before it goes out.
