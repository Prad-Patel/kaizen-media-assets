Did you know the UK government will teach you AI for free? 👀

Any UK adult can take short AI courses on the government's AI Skills Hub, and some take as little as 20 minutes. You get a virtual badge at the end.

Before you pick one, check:
1. Is it short enough to finish today?
2. Does it match a job you do every week?
3. Who built it?
4. Does it meet the Skills England benchmark?
5. What will you use it on straight after?
