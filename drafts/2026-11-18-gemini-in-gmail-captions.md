## LinkedIn (personal profile; reach test 1 opener = concrete number; no links; no firstComment at publish per reach test 3)

117 emails a day. That is Microsoft's figure for the average worker, and most get skimmed in under a minute.

If your business runs on Google Workspace, Gemini in Gmail can now take two of the slowest jobs off that pile. Google started switching it on for Workspace accounts on 10 November.

Here is how we'd set it up for a client:

1. Check smart features are on (Settings, See all settings, General).
2. Open a long thread and read the AI Overview at the top instead of all twelve messages.
3. Select Reply, then Help me write, and give it the facts: who, what, when, price, word limit.
4. Type rough notes ("can do tues not weds, price same") and choose Polish. It turns them into a proper email.

On our own rough maths, 15 minutes a day back is about 60 hours a year. Read every draft before it goes. Gemini drafts, you decide.
