A Nintendo Switch 2 is already £30 off at Amazon UK and Very this week, down from £419.99 to £389.99, and Black Friday is still three days away. Prices on the presents you want will move up and down between now and Christmas, and most of us either miss the dip or spend evenings refreshing product pages. You do not have to. Grok, the AI assistant from xAI, can check the prices on your whole present list every morning and send a notification to your phone when one drops. Here is how we would set it up tonight, with the prompt to copy.

Why it is worth ten minutes this week

Christmas prices are not fixed. Deals come and go across Black Friday, Cyber Monday and the run-up to Christmas, and the same item can be a different price at different retailers on the same day. The Switch 2 shows it: Creative Bloq reported the console at £389.99 at Amazon UK and Very, a £30 cut, while Which? says it has tracked it as low as £355 over the past six months and warns that several bundles save nothing at all. Knowing the normal price, and being told the moment it drops below it, is where the money is.

Doing that by hand for five or six presents is tedious. An assistant that runs on a schedule does it while you get on with your day.

What Grok can do here

In July xAI launched Grok Automations (it used to be called Tasks). You write a prompt once and Grok runs it on a schedule, from hourly to daily, weekly or monthly, and can send the result to you as a phone notification, an email or both. Scheduled automations are available to all users at no extra cost, according to launch coverage. Grok can search the web when it runs, so a scheduled prompt can look up today's price for each item on your list.

One honest note: Grok is not a dedicated price tracker, and it reads prices from what it finds online rather than from a retailer's checkout. Treat its alert as a tip-off, then check the price on the retailer's own site before you buy.

Set it up in four steps

Step 1: Write your list

Note each present with the exact model, the retailers you would happily buy from and the price you would be pleased to pay. Be specific. "Nintendo Switch 2 console, standard edition, from Amazon UK, Argos, Currys, John Lewis or Very, target £370" works far better than "a Switch".

Step 2: Start a new automation in Grok

Open Grok in the app or on the web and look for Automations (or Tasks, depending on your version). Start a new one. The layout is still changing, so the button names in your account may differ slightly.

Step 3: Paste this prompt

You are my Christmas price watcher. Every time you run, search UK retailers for today's price of each item below. For each item, tell me: the lowest price you can find today, which retailer it is at, the usual price over the past few months if you can find it, and whether today's price is at or below my target. Only check UK retailers and prices in pounds, including delivery where it is shown. If any item is at or below my target, put it at the top of your reply with the word DEAL and the link. If nothing is at target, keep the reply to one line per item. My list: 1. Nintendo Switch 2 console, standard edition, target £370. 2. [item], target £[price]. 3. [item], target £[price].

Step 4: Schedule it and switch on alerts

Set it to run every morning, say 7am, and turn on phone notifications. Daily is enough most of the year. From Thursday night to Cyber Monday, when deals move fastest, you could switch it to every few hours, then back to daily until Christmas. When a DEAL alert arrives, open the link, check the price, the seller and the returns policy, and buy.

What it could save you

An illustration, on our own assumptions. Say you have five bigger presents on the list and Grok catches a dip of around £30 on each, which is the size of the Switch 2 cut already on offer this week. That is about £150 back across the season, for ten minutes of setup and a glance at your phone. Some items will not drop at all, and some will drop by more, so treat that as a ballpark, not a promise.

Not on Grok? The same trick works in ChatGPT

ChatGPT has its own scheduled tasks, and guides this month show a ready-made price-drop task you can set up from its Scheduled menu. The prompt above works there too. Which plans get scheduled tasks, and how many, has changed several times, so check the Scheduled page in your own account.

Three things to watch

  • Check before you buy. AI can misread a price, pick up a marketplace seller instead of the retailer, or miss a delivery charge. The alert tells you where to look; the retailer's page is the price.
  • Know the real "was" price. A big "was" figure is not proof of a deal. Asking Grok for the usual price over recent months is what stops you paying a Black Friday price that was the same in October.
  • Switch it off after Christmas. Automations keep running until you stop them. Delete it once the presents are wrapped.
