## LinkedIn (personal profile, PLAIN TEXT this time per reach test 4; no firstComment at publish, links added by hand about an hour later)

ChatGPT just gave business owners something Google gave us years ago: a listing you can claim.

OpenAI launched Businesses in ChatGPT yesterday. Verify that you own your business and you can correct what ChatGPT says about you, then see how often it mentions you, how many clicks it sends to your website and what people were asking about.

The UK catch: if you have a website, you can very likely claim today. Local shops and restaurants can only claim existing US listings for now.

It takes about ten minutes:
1. Open Business Hub from OpenAI's Businesses in ChatGPT page
2. Verify by business phone, business email, a TXT record on your domain, or Shopify
3. Check your name, description and services match your Google profile
4. Note your mentions and clicks, then check again in a month

Two things worth knowing: claim it before someone else tries to, and OpenAI says paying for ads does not change how often it recommends you.

Have you asked ChatGPT what it says about your business yet?
