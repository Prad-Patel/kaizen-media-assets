## LinkedIn (personal profile, carousel PDF; Kaizen page reshares)

We built the equivalent of a £20,000 platform in one month, using Claude as the main builder: a matching tool, a CRM and a brand kit. Tool costs: about £500 to build and about £90 a month to run.

The idea was simple. Sellers pick an estate agent on fees and brochures, but the thing that shapes the sale is the person. So sellers answer questions about their sale and the personality they want in an agent, and the tool matches them to the agent who fits. Behind it sits a CRM so agents can track every lead, and a brand kit that keeps our marketing consistent.

In our own estimate, a first version built the traditional way would have been the equivalent of a £20,000 build and several months. That is our judgement, not a published figure. The agents involved have given strong feedback, and several leads have come through so far, most of which went on to instruct. It is a small sample, but it is a real signal.

This is our own venture, so your costs and results will differ. If you have an idea you want to bring to life, get in touch.
