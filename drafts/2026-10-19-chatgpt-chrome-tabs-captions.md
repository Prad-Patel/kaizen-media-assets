## LinkedIn

We would not switch this on without one rule: give it its own browser profile.

OpenAI's 15 October update lets ChatGPT read your open Chrome tabs. In its Work mode, with your permission, it can also fill in forms, send messages and download files across them.

That is tempting for the admin that eats a small business week (the Amex SME Barometer puts it at about 11 hours for UK owners). It also means an assistant acting as you, inside every account you are logged into.

The checklist we would give a client:
1. A clean Chrome profile, signed in only to the sites the job needs
2. Allow one site, once
3. Start with a low-stakes form
4. Read every field, then press submit yourself

We have not tested it on a client setup yet, so this is a precaution, not a review.
