## LinkedIn (personal profile, carousel PDF; Kaizen page reshares)

Fifteen minutes of set-up can end the daily ritual of introducing your business to an AI.

Here is the method, in plain steps:

1. Create a Claude Project and name it after one job, such as customer emails.
2. In a normal chat, ask Claude to interview you with up to eight questions and write a briefing of 300 words or less.
3. Paste that briefing into the project instructions.
4. Add two or three files: a price list, an FAQ, a few replies you were pleased with. Remove customer names first.
5. Open a new chat inside the project and test it with a real request. If it misses, fix the instructions, not the chat.

The copy-paste prompt for step 2 is on slide 3. As an illustration, five minutes a day of re-explaining is about 20 hours over 48 working weeks. Time your own week to check.

One thing to watch: plan limits change, so check what your own account offers.
