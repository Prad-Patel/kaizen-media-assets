## LinkedIn (personal profile, carousel PDF)

Claude Sheets will now build a full Christmas staff rota from one message, with everyone's hours totalled by formula and every short shift listed for you.

That matters in December. Legion's UK research found 65% of retail managers already spend more than three hours a week on scheduling, before the late nights, holiday requests and two bank holidays land.

How we'd set it up today:

1. Jot down names, contracted hours, requests, opening hours and staff per shift.
2. Paste one prompt that asks for three tabs: Rota, Hours and Gaps.
3. Answer its questions before it builds.
4. When someone calls in sick, ask who can cover. Don't rebuild.
5. Download it as Excel and send it the way you always do.

The full prompt is on slide 4. It's a beta on paid plans, so check the totals once before you trust them.
