## LinkedIn (Prad personal profile, video)
1 million people missed the Self Assessment deadline last January. Each one started with an automatic £100 penalty.

For accountants, January isn't short of skill. It's short of paperwork. The P60 that never arrived. The rental figures in a shoebox.

So we built an accounting firm a tool that does the chasing. With Claude, in about 2 hours.

Every client on one list. Paste them in from Excel. Reminders get closer together as 31 January nears, and the wording moves from friendly to urgent to overdue on its own. One button each morning sends the day's emails and logs every one. Test mode is on by default, so nothing reaches a client until the firm is ready.

To be straight: it isn't live with clients yet, so there are no results to share. The video uses demo data.

Our rough maths: 50 slow clients x 5 chasers x 3 minutes is about 12.5 hours in the busiest weeks of the year.

Accountants and bookkeepers: how many hours does your team lose to chasing paperwork each January?
