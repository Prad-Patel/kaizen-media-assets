## LinkedIn (Prad personal profile, carousel PDF; no links in body)

An earlier version of this tool generated £7m worth of leads for one of our clients in 11 months.

So we rebuilt it properly. Worktop Compare is a price calculator for stone worktops with a live 3D preview: type in your measurements and your own layout builds on screen, ready to rotate and zoom.

A few years ago that 3D view alone was a $15,000 to $50,000 specialist job. We built the whole platform in one month with Claude.

But the real win is the lead. Instead of "how much for a quartz worktop?", the fabricator gets stone, brand, colour, every measurement, edge profile and extras, from a homeowner who has already seen an estimated price. Each enquiry goes to up to 5 local fabricators who cover that postcode and work with that stone.

It is not live yet. Any business that quotes made-to-measure work could use the same idea.
