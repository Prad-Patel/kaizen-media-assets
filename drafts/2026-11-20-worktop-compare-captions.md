## LinkedIn (Prad personal profile, carousel PDF; no links in body)

An earlier version of this tool generated £7m worth of leads for one of our clients in 11 months.

So we rebuilt it. Worktop Compare is an instant price calculator for kitchen worktops, and it took us one month with Claude, Lovable, Supabase, Apify and ChatGPT.

The old way to buy a worktop: email a floor plan, wait, book a site visit just to get a number, then do it all again with two more suppliers.

The new way:
1. Answer a few questions about your kitchen and get an indication of the cost straight away.
2. Get matched to suppliers who can give you the best value.

Suppliers get warm leads from people who have already seen a real number. Homeowners get a realistic budget before anyone visits.

It is not live yet. But any business that answers "how much would it be?" every week could use the same idea.
