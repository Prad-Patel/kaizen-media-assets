ChatGPT now lets you lock a conversation behind a four-digit PIN, and for a small business it is a small feature with a practical payoff. OpenAI began rolling out Locked Chats on 20 October 2026. A locked chat disappears from your normal history and search, and only opens when you enter the PIN (or Face ID or Touch ID on a supported phone). If you have ever handed a laptop to a colleague, or left a work phone on a desk, and then remembered what was sitting in your ChatGPT sidebar, this is the feature for you. It is also not a vault, and the gap between those two things is what this post is about.

What Locked Chats actually does

According to TechCrunch's report on the launch, you can seal individual conversations behind a PIN, Face ID or Touch ID. Locked chats are removed from your regular chat history, your pinned chats and search, and they sit in their own section under Projects. On the web and desktop they lock again when you refresh the page, close the tab or step away from the window, so you are not leaving a door propped open by accident.

There is a second benefit that is easy to miss. When Memory is switched on, locked chats get a memory of their own, shared across the chats in that locked section. New messages in a locked chat update that private memory rather than your regular one. So a sensitive conversation about, say, a staffing problem is less likely to leak into the suggestions and personalisation you see in everyday chats. Android Authority's write-up covers the same mechanics from the phone side.

How to lock a chat in under a minute

  1. Open the chat you want to protect.
  2. Click or tap the three dots (the more options menu) on that chat.
  3. Choose Lock chat and confirm.
  4. The first time, ChatGPT asks you to create a four-digit PIN. Enter it twice.
  5. Find your locked chats later under Projects, then Locked chats, and unlock with the PIN.

Rollout is gradual, so you may not see the option yet. Coverage also disagrees on which plans get it: some reports list Free, Go, Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Edu, others name only the consumer plans. If it is missing on a work account, check with whoever manages your ChatGPT workspace before assuming you have done something wrong.

What you gain as a small business owner

Most small firms share devices more than they admit: a family laptop, a tablet in the van, a work phone passed to a new starter. On those, the main thing Locked Chats buys you is that a casual glance at your ChatGPT history no longer shows everything you have asked. A few jobs where that tidiness is worth having:

  • Drafting a difficult email to a customer or a member of staff, before you decide whether to send it.
  • Working through your pricing and margins, which you may not want visible to everyone who borrows your screen.
  • Sorting out a personal or HR matter where you want to think out loud with an assistant first.
  • Keeping a clean sidebar: moving the sensitive chats out means the history you do see is the working stuff you actually want to find again.

That is the gain: less exposure to a nosy glance, and a tidier, more useful history. It is a convenience feature that happens to help with privacy, and it is worth using for exactly that.

What stays visible, and what it does not do

Here is the part we would tell any client before they start relying on it. OpenAI says locking does not create end-to-end encryption or a separate encrypted vault, and it does not change how long OpenAI keeps the chat. Several other things are worth knowing:

  • Forgetting the PIN is expensive. If you reset it, OpenAI permanently deletes all your locked chats, along with files and information stored only in those conversations. Support cannot bring them back.
  • Your training setting does not change. Locking leaves the "Improve the model for everyone" choice exactly where it was, so check that setting separately.
  • Earlier memories stay. Locking a conversation does not erase anything ChatGPT remembered about you before the chat was locked.
  • Old shared links keep working. If you shared a chat before locking it, the link is not revoked. Delete the link yourself.
  • Some chats cannot be locked. Temporary chats, archived chats and group chats are out, and so are Codex and local Work conversations.
  • Copying is still possible. Locked chats cannot be pinned, archived, moved or shared, but anyone who opens one can still copy the text.

We have not tested Locked Chats ourselves yet, and these details come from OpenAI's launch coverage rather than hands-on use, so read OpenAI's own release notes alongside this post and check the behaviour on your own account before you rely on it.

What we would tell a client

A PIN lock answers one question: who can see this on my screen? It does not answer the other one: what have I told an AI service? Those are different risks, and only the first one is solved here. So our advice is simple.

  1. Treat the lock as a curtain, not a safe. Use it for tidiness and casual privacy. If a piece of information should never leave your business, do not paste it into a chat at all, locked or not.
  2. Store the PIN somewhere sensible. Put it in your password manager the moment you set it. Losing it costs you every locked chat, permanently.
  3. Take two minutes to review your other settings. Look at the model-training toggle, your saved memories and any old shared links. Those are the things Locked Chats leaves untouched.
  4. Keep personal and work chats apart. A locked section is a good home for the genuinely sensitive ones. Everything else can stay in the open sidebar where you will find it.

Try this one this afternoon

Open your ChatGPT history and scroll back through the last month. Pick the two or three chats you would least like a colleague to stumble on, lock them, and set a PIN you will remember. Then spend a minute in settings checking the training toggle and deleting any shared links you no longer need. That is about five minutes, and you finish with a cleaner sidebar and a clearer idea of what ChatGPT does and does not protect.
